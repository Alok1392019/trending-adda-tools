Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

1. Absolute Client-Side Security Guarantee

At **Trending Adda Tools**, privacy is not a feature—it is our core architectural foundation. Unlike traditional utility web portals that transmit your texts, uploaded photos, spreadsheets, or PDF packages to remote cloud networks for conversion, **all our tools run 100% locally on your computer or mobile device**.

Because all computing takes place directly inside your own web browser tab via HTML5 Canvas, File API, and local JavaScript scripts, your sensitive documents never leave your system. Your data remains fully secure, private, and inaccessible to anyone, including ourselves.

2. What Information We Do NOT Collect

* **No Document Storage:** We never store, log, inspect, or backup any images, scripts, or PDF files you upload to our utilities. * **No Text Logging:** Standard text inputs pasted in our text counters, font tools, or social generators are never logged. * **No Database Storage:** There are no backend database servers tracking your files or values.
